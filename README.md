# Live Salary Counter

A simple HTML-based tool that tracks your live earnings or the live cost of a meeting. You can try it out here: [Live Salary Counter](https://acse-dc1823.github.io/live-salary-counter/).

## Features

- **Track Live Earnings**: Input your annual salary and watch your earnings grow in real time.
- **Multiple Participants**: Add multiple participants to calculate the live cost of a meeting.
- **Customizable Modes**:
  - **Overall Mode**: Calculates salary as if you're earning every second of the year.
  - **Hourly Mode**: Adjusts calculations to reflect only your working hours (e.g., 40 hours per week).

## How to Use

1. Open the tool: [Live Salary Counter](https://acse-dc1823.github.io/live-salary-counter/).
2. Add participants by inputting their yearly salaries.
3. Choose whether to enable "Hourly Mode" and specify weekly hours worked, if applicable.
4. Start the counter to see live earnings or meeting costs.
5. Pause, stop, or calculate projected costs as needed.

## Disclaimer

This tool does not store or transmit any of your data. All calculations happen locally in your browser for complete privacy.
